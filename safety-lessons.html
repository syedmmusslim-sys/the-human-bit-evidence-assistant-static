Safety lessons carried forward without turning the product into a proof harness.

Later hardening work produced useful safety lessons, but those lessons belong in diligence. The buyer journey remains the staff/reviewer/manager close workflow.

Human review stays in control

  • Staff intake never equals accepted evidence.
  • Corrected evidence moves to waiting review.
  • Reviewer action is required before packet readiness changes.
  • Rejected, stale, missing, wrong-period or ambiguous evidence cannot silently become accepted.

Chase and packet rules

  • Accepted evidence is not re-chased.
  • Unresolved client-owned blockers remain on the chase list.
  • Manager handoff remains cannot-close while valid blockers remain.
  • The pilot package sells workflow control, not AI prose quality.

Safety boundary

  • This public artifact has no form, upload, email send, mutable API or client-data intake.
  • Real client data requires written permission, deployment controls, retention/deletion terms and private-pilot approval.
  • No production SaaS, live integration, tenant auth or strict-header hosting claim is made here.

Private-pilot lessons not shown as product

  • Backend/auth/persistence hardening remains a future private-pilot engineering track.
  • Those learnings do not replace this public buyer package.
  • No later proof-harness page is allowed to define public product quality.
