Build 81 pilot conversion packet: who approves, what happens, what is measured.

This is a static public handoff, not a form. A qualified firm can decide whether to request a supervised two-close-cycle concierge pilot without entering client data here.

Named approval path

Buyer: CAS/bookkeeping partner or manager responsible for monthly close quality. Approval step: email The Human Bit founder with “Evidence Assistant supervised pilot” and receive a private setup call plus DPA/security checklist before any client material is shared.

Duration and effort

Scope: two close cycles, one workflow, up to ten recurring evidence blockers. Firm effort: one 45-minute setup, 15 minutes per close day for blocker review, one 30-minute retrospective.

Price stance

Concierge pilot: paid discovery pilot only after security/DPA approval; price is intentionally proposal-based until buyer validation exists. No self-serve checkout and no production subscription claim.

Success criteria

Measure duplicate chase reduction, reviewer rework avoided, unresolved blockers visible before manager review, and whether the manager would rely on the handoff packet at close.

Security / DPA checklist before any pilot

  • No client uploads to this public artifact.
  • Confirm data categories, retention, deletion and access owner.
  • Use anonymised/sample evidence until written approval.
  • Reviewer-only accept/reject remains mandatory; no auto-send or auto-accept.
  • Define incident contact, export requirement and offboarding path.
