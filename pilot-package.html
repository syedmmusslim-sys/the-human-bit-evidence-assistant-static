Concierge pilot offer for firms that already feel close pain.

This is a supervised design-partner package, not production SaaS. The buyer can judge fit before sharing real client data.

Who qualifies

  • Accounting/bookkeeping/CAS firm with recurring monthly close evidence chases.
  • One sponsor, one staff operator, one reviewer and one manager available.
  • Willing to run 1–3 redacted/synthetic clients over two close cycles.

Who should not start yet

  • Needs unattended live client data today.
  • Requires production identity, integrations or live email sending before pilot.
  • Cannot provide reviewer time or baseline close friction measures.

What the firm gets

  • Seeded evidence-state workbench.
  • Guided chase list and reviewer queue simulation.
  • Manager packet readiness review.
  • Two-cycle outcome review and go/no-go recommendation.

Static CTA

Next step: book a 30-minute qualification call and bring one anonymised close checklist plus three recent evidence chase examples. No public form or email send is embedded in this artifact.
