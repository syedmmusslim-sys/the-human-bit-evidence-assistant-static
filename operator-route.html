This page turns the demo into a concrete proof story: what close looked like before the workflow, what changed after staff intake, reviewer decision, chase and manager handoff.

Before — 08:10

Cannot close because: payroll clearing evidence is missing; bank rec support is waiting review; GST adjustment support was rejected as unreadable. The manager cannot see which items are safe to rely on.

Staff intake — 08:22

Client reply received: bank rec PDF and GST screenshot. The workflow marks bank rec as waiting review and GST as rejected/problem with reason “cropped period/date”.

Reviewer decision — 08:41

Reviewer accepts bank rec support, rejects cropped GST screenshot, and leaves payroll clearing as requested/missing. The accepted item moves into evidence-pack-ready; rejected and missing items remain chaseable.

Chase — 08:47

Draft chase includes only unresolved blockers: payroll clearing evidence and GST screenshot with visible period/date. It excludes accepted bank rec support and remains staff-approved before simulated send.

After — 09:05

Manager handoff: one accepted item, two unresolved blockers, reviewer reasons, client chase trail and explicit “cannot close because…” summary. No AI acceptance or send claim.
