Run a safe walkthrough — not a data transfer.

Use this as the commercial next step: a 15–30 minute sample-data walkthrough before any client files are discussed.

Firm-fit checklist

  • 20–50 person CAS/bookkeeping firm or close team.
  • Recurring evidence chases across portal/email/shared drives.
  • Manager cannot quickly say “can/cannot close because…”.
  • Reviewer rework and duplicate asks are visible enough to measure.

Required attendees

  • Partner/sponsor who owns close quality.
  • Staff operator who chases evidence.
  • Reviewer who accepts/rejects support.
  • Manager who signs off the packet.

No-client-files gate

First call uses only this synthetic Blue Wattle sample. Real/redacted data requires DPA, access control, retention/deletion, and security agreement first.
